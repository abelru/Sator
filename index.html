How to play

Fill the grid so every row is a real word, and each row matches the column with the same number. Row 2 reads the same as column 2, row 3 as column 3, and so on.

You start with the first word filled in across and down. Every letter you type also lands in its mirror spot (row 2, column 4 matches row 4, column 2), so you’re really choosing words, not letters.

Tap a square in the top-right half to type down its column, or in the bottom-left half to type across its row. Tap the same square again to switch direction.

The puzzle finishes on its own as soon as every row is a real word. Before that, press Check to test your rows. The dots beside each row and under each column show the result:

✓
A real word. Squares where two real words cross turn green.
×
Not in the word list. Its letters turn red.
!
No word fits the letters so far (you can turn this off in settings; solves with it on don’t go on the leaderboard).

Any valid word counts. Your square doesn’t have to match ours. Stuck? Hint fills in a word that works with the letters you already have. Puzzles solved with hints get a yellow dot instead of a green one.

Rare bonus: if the diagonal from the top-left corner to the bottom-right also spells a word when you finish, it lights up gold.

Daily gives everyone the same puzzle for each size, with a new one at midnight. Unlimited lets you keep going. Sizes 3 to 6 use everyday words; the Expert boards are 7 and 8, which need rarer words, and the 9×9, a single legendary square that stays put until you crack it.

Free play takes away the starting word, so you build the whole square yourself at any size. It’s just for fun: free play never goes on the leaderboard.
